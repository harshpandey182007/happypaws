About Us

Happy Paws Pet Care started as a small neighbourhood clinic run by a group of animal lovers. Today we are a friendly pet care center where dogs, cats and other pets get gentle, professional care.

Our team of vets, groomers and trainers treats every pet like family. We use gentle, safe products and take time to understand each animal's personality.
